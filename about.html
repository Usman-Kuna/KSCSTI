About the Institute

The Kano State Corporate Security Training Institute (KSCSTI) is a state-owned center committed to providing modern, practical, and globally relevant training in the field of corporate and private security. Our programs combine academic instruction with practical drills and fieldwork to produce well-rounded professionals ready to serve in public and private sectors.

Our Mission

To deliver top-quality training and certifications that build integrity, leadership and technical competence in security personnel.

Our Vision

To be the foremost security training institute in the region, recognized for excellence, discipline and community partnership.

Programs & Certification

Basic Security Training

Foundational course for new recruits.

Advanced Security Management

Leadership and operations for supervisors and managers.

Technical Surveillance

CCTV, monitoring, and digital evidence handling.
