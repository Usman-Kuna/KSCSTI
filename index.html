About the Institute

The Kano State Corporate Security Training Institute (KSCSTI) is a state-owned center dedicated to training and developing competent, ethical, and professional security personnel. Located in Gabasawa, Kano State, the Institute blends theory and field practice to produce leaders in corporate and community security management.
